Project brief

Scope

The research converts motion-tracking data into flight descriptions that can separate takeoff, landing, turning, and steady motion before frequency and force relationships are examined.

01Reconstruct trajectories and wing motion from tracked data in MATLAB.
02Classify flight regimes such as takeoff, landing, and turning from the reconstructed motion.
03Use FFT analysis and Newtonian force relationships to study flapping frequency, lift, and drag or thrust behavior.
