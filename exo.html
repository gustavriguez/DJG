Project brief

Scope

The design work turns actuator and human-body constraints into a mechanical layout that can be adjusted, assembled, worn, and revised as the system develops.

01Package actuators and structure around hip and knee joint alignment rather than forcing the body around the hardware.
02Use anthropometric ranges to drive adjustability, clearances, and fit decisions.
03Balance load paths, comfort, manufacturability, and access for assembly and service.
