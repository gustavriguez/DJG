Scope

The car began as a stripped project and ended as a finished driver. The work crossed nearly every major assembly, with most fabrication and fitting done using garage-scale tools.

02Converted the front braking system from drums to discs.
03Installed a Mustang II front suspension package and reworked the front-end layout around it.
04Cut, repaired, fitted, and refinished body panels.
05Reassembled engine-bay hardware and vehicle wiring around the updated mechanical package.
06Installed sound-deadening material and fabricated a custom center console.
