The shell was developed in stages so the outside shape stayed recognizable without trapping the hardware inside. The printed body keeps the motors, controller, sensor, and wiring accessible.

01Established the body and wheel envelope in the first CAD pass.
02Added exterior geometry and adjusted proportions around the drive hardware.
03Prepared the final shell for printing and electronics installation.
04Installed dual motors, controller hardware, sensor wiring, and LED feedback.
05Painted and assembled the final unit for K–12 engineering outreach.
